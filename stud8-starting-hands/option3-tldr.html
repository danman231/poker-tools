Option 3: TL;DR Summary

Bold summary at top, details below in lighter text

Trips
Quick Take
Max value on 3rd street. Build the pot.
Full Notes
  • On 3rd get as much money into the pot as possible.
Two Aces
Quick Take
Low kicker preferred. Keep it heads-up for scoops. Split/buried aces disguise your hand.
Full Notes
  • Having low kicker preferred
  • If Aces are split or buried, opponent will have hard time knowing which way we are going.
  • Short handed pots will produce more scoops
  • High kickers has less value and when aces are buried, everyone knows we're going high.
    • May not want to reraise if there are many players in the pot. Can see what develops on 4th and maybe make a snug fold.
3 Suited Low Cards
Quick Take
Check suit liveness. Dead suits = one-way hand only.
Full Notes
  • If many of your suit is out, you are playing a one way hand.
Kings and Queens
Quick Take
Heads-up only. Fold to Ace completing from non-steal position. QQ folds to KK.
Full Notes
  • High pairs are good if:
    • exposed cards and action suggests we have best high hand
    • Pot doesn't go multiway
  • Playing against many low cards can get rough and we can get jammed on in later streets.
  • Should fold against an Ace that completes from a non steal position.
  • Pair of queens should be folded when a king completes.
  • Sometimes make tight folds when there is an ace up card behind
  • In early position, best to fold when ace and many other low cards left to act.
  • Many opponents with an Ace will reraise many hands that don't currently beat a pair of kings, so only fold to opponents playing straight forward.
  • Very aggressive players will take off with (2-J)A and pick up pair and small draw along the way. Calling down is high variance, but we need to accept this situation.
Jacks, Tens, Nines
Quick Take
Fold EP. Open-complete in MP/LP with clear board. Buried pairs (JJ)3 can take more risk.
Full Notes
  • Should fold in early position
  • Mostly playable as open complete when in middle or later position & not many threatening cards behind.
  • Most all cards left to act are a threat. Hand doesn't play well multiway and when a low bricks, they usually catch an overcard to our pair.
  • Fold to a complete from a higher up pair
  • Even if Ace is betting in a steal spot and still bring in left to act, can be dicey.
  • If our kicker is an Ace or another ace already folded, equity goes up and you should reraise.
  • If hand is buried and have a low card up like (J-J)3 we can assume more risk.
