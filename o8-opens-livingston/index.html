Livingston Notation

ss Single-suited
ds Double-suited
rainbow All four suits different
$W$W / W Wheel card (A, 2, 3, 4, 5)
:xx Single-suited modifier
:xxyy Double-suited modifier
:Kxx K + single-suit + extra card
! "Not" prefix (e.g. !ss = not single-suited)

Examples From Livingston's Slides

KKQQss KK + QQ single-suited — premium high-only open.
KQJTds Broadway run, double-suited — top high-only opener.
KK:$W$W KK with two wheel cards — always opens.
QQ43ss Pocket queens + 4-3, single-suited — LJ open.
KQ32:Kxx KQ32 with the K suited to one of the side cards.
Axx!xyy!(AA,2,3,4,5) Ace-x single-suit, not double-suited, no AA/wheel.
