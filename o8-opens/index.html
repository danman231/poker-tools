O8

Opening Ranges

Full Ring Low-Oriented

ALL Open all combos
NONE Don't open
TT+ Specific combos only

Positions

LJ Lojack (UTG in 6-max)
HJ Hijack
CO Cutoff
BU Button

Hand Notation

B Broadway card (T, J, Q, K, A)
DS Double suited
PP Pocket pair
X Any random card
(XY) Cards in parentheses are suited together
T Ten
+ That card or higher / that pair or better
(s) Suited with the Ace

Examples

A2(2X) Ace-deuce with the deuce suited to a random card
A(4J)J+ Ace-four with JJ+, where the 4 and one Jack are suited. JJ+ means Jacks or better (JJ, QQ, KK, AA)
(A4)5B Ace-four suited together, with a 5 and a Broadway card
44-55(s) Pocket fours or fives, suited with another card (typically the Ace)
TT+ Tens or better as a pocket pair (TT, JJ, QQ, KK, AA)
T+ Ten or higher as a single card (T, J, Q, K, A)
